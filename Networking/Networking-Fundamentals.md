# Networking Fundamentals

# What is a Computer Network?

A computer network is a group of devices connected together to share information and resources.

# Types of Networks

## LAN (Local Area Network)

A LAN connects devices within a small geographical area, such as a home, office, or university campus.

## MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

A MAN connects multiple Local Area Networks (LANs) across a city or metropolitan area.

## WAN (Wide Area Network)

A WAN connects networks over large geographical distances, such as between cities, countries, or continents. The Internet is the largest example of a WAN.

# Why Networking Matters

Networking allows devices to:

- Communicate with each other
- Share files and resources
- Access the Internet
- Use shared services such as printers and servers

# Skills Learned

- IP Addressing
- Subnetting
- OSI Model
- TCP/IP Model
- Common Network Protocols
- Basic Network Security

# Key Takeaway

Networking is the foundation of cybersecurity. Before securing a network, you must understand how devices communicate, how data travels, and how networks are designed.
